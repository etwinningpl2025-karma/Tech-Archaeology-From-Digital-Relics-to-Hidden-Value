About the Project

Merging STEAM education, electronic archaeology, and environmental action

Yesterday's Tech, Tomorrow's Potential

In an accelerating digital world, discarded electronics pile up as forgotten digital relics. Tech-Archaeology challenges students to investigate the lifecycle of hardware, dissect internal components, and champion circular economy mindsets through cross-border collaboration.

Merging STEAM education, 3D photogrammetry, and environmental advocacy, our young innovators uncover the hidden value inside e-waste.

Five Action Pillars

Pedagogical foundation guiding our project outcomes

Explore the Past

Examining the evolution of circuitry, obsolete standards, and retro hardware.

Investigate Present

Auditing personal digital footprints and raw electronic consumption rates.

Find Hidden Value

Extracting components, upcycling parts, and crafting circular artistic outputs.

Learn Together

Cross-border teams tackling shared eco-technological design briefs.

Sustainable Future

Inspiring proactive green consumer habits and school-wide e-waste collection.

Roadmap & Key Activities

Structured 4-month collaborative STEAM agenda

Month 1 • Nov

Digital Excavation & Disassembly

Students step into the shoes of digital archaeologists, taking apart broken hardware to inspect internal anatomy and building a physical/virtual 'Tech Museum'.

Hardware Anatomy Tech Museum
Month 2 • Dec

E-Waste & Upcycling Art

International mixed squads repurpose electronic components into collaborative sculptures, digitized via modern 3D scanning workflows.

Polycam Canva Mixed Teams
Month 3 • Jan

AI & Eco-Tech Hackathon

Students craft targeted generative AI prompts to design sustainable hardware for 2050, accompanied by a structured Digital Detox challenge.

AI Prompting Eco-Design Digital Detox
Month 4 • Feb

Virtual Gallery & Local Impact

Curating an interactive 3D virtual exhibition to showcase student projects, paired with a concrete school-wide e-waste collection campaign.

Spatial Artsteps E-Waste Drive
